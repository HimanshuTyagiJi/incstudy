CCSU BCA Examination Structure & Marking Pattern

Theory examinations conducted by Chaudhary Charan Singh University follow an established marking criteria designed to test theoretical clarity, analytical calculation, and descriptive answer writing:

  • Total Marks per Theory Paper: 100 Marks (75 Marks External University Examination + 25 Marks Internal College Sessional Assessment).
  • External Theory Exam Duration: 3 Hours.
  • Section A (Compulsory): 5 very short questions carrying 3 marks each (Total: 15 marks). Answers are expected in 50–75 words covering exact definitions, syntax rules, and truth tables across all 5 syllabus units.
  • Section B (Short Analytical): Attempt any 2 out of 3 questions carrying 7.5 marks each (Total: 15 marks). Requires flowcharts, memory dry-run tables, circuit schematics, or intermediate derivations.
  • Section C (Descriptive Long Answers): Attempt any 3 out of 5 comprehensive questions carrying 15 marks each (Total: 45 marks). Represents 60% of the theory score and requires complete programs, system block diagrams, and detailed architectural explanations.
  • Passing Standard: A student must secure a minimum aggregate of 40% across theory and sessional assessments to pass each subject.
