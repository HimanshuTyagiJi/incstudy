CCSU BCA 3rd Semester Exam Pattern & Blueprint (600 Marks)

In Semester 3, the curriculum transitions into fundamental computer science engineering subjects: Object-Oriented paradigm, complex non-linear data structures, and computer organization. The semester carries a total of 600 Marks (5 Theory papers × 100M = 500M, and 2 Practical Lab courses × 50M = 100M).

Key Scoring Tip for 3rd Sem: In Data Structures (BCA-302), always write step-by-step algorithms, draw stack/queue/tree memory diagrams, and mention Time Complexity \(O(n)\) for Section C (15 marks) questions.
