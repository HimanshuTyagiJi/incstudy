CCSU BCA 2nd Semester Examination Blueprint

Official University Theory Marking Pattern

External University Theory (75 Marks) + Internal Assessment (25 Marks) for BCA-201 to BCA-205
Duration: 3 Hours

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (Mandatory)

5 Qs × 3 Marks = 15 Marks

All 5 questions are compulsory across units — concise definitions, truth tables, C syntax checks, and short derivations.

Section B: Short Answer Questions

Attempt 2 out of 3 Qs = 15 Marks

Analytical and code-tracing questions covering C functions, K-Maps, combinational circuits, and accounting entries (7.5 marks each).

Section C: Detailed Long Answer Questions

Attempt 3 out of 5 Qs = 45 Marks

In-depth theoretical and numerical problems — complete C programs, sequential circuits, graph proofs, and final accounts (15 marks each). Practical paper BCA-206 is evaluated separately through coding execution and external viva.
