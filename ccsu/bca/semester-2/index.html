CCSU BCA 2nd Semester Examination Blueprint

Official University Theory Marking Pattern

External University Theory (75 Marks) + Internal Assessment (25 Marks) for BCA-201 to BCA-205
Duration: 3 Hours

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (Mandatory)

5 Qs × 3 Marks = 15 Marks

All 5 questions are compulsory across units — concise definitions, truth tables, C syntax checks, and short derivations.

Section B: Short Answer Questions

Attempt 2 out of 3 Qs = 15 Marks

Analytical and code-tracing questions covering C functions, K-Maps, combinational circuits, and accounting entries (7.5 marks each).

Section C: Detailed Long Answer Questions

Attempt 3 out of 5 Qs = 45 Marks

In-depth theoretical and numerical problems — complete C programs, sequential circuits, graph proofs, and final accounts (15 marks each). Practical paper BCA-206 is evaluated separately through coding execution and external viva.

Detailed Unit-Wise Syllabus & Subject Guide

1. BCA-201: Mathematics-II (Discrete Mathematics & Sets)

Builds logical precision for computer science, focusing on discrete mathematical structures:

  • Unit I (Sets & Relations): Set operations, power sets, Cartesian products, equivalence relations, partitions, partial orderings, and Hasse diagrams.
  • Unit II (Functions & Pigeonhole Principle): Injective, surjective, bijective functions, composition of functions, recursive functions, and the Pigeonhole principle with proofs.
  • Unit III (Mathematical Logic & Propositional Calculus): Truth tables, tautologies, contradictions, logical equivalences, normal forms (DNF, CNF), and predicate logic quantifiers.
  • Unit IV (Lattices & Boolean Algebra): Posets as lattices, distributive and complemented lattices, Boolean expressions, and Karnaugh map minimization.
  • Unit V (Graph Theory & Trees): Undirected/directed graphs, Eulerian and Hamiltonian paths, planar graphs, trees, spanning trees, and Prim's/Kruskal's algorithm.
  • Recommended Textbook: Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications by Kenneth H. Rosen (McGraw Hill).

2. BCA-202: Advanced C-Programming & Data Concepts

Expands upon procedural foundations into modular systems programming and dynamic memory:

  • Unit I (Advanced Pointers & Memory): Pointer to pointer, pointer arithmetic, void pointers, dynamic memory allocation functions (malloc, calloc, realloc, free).
  • Unit II (Strings & Multi-dimensional Arrays): 2D array memory representation (Row-major vs Column-major), string handling algorithms without standard library functions, arrays of pointers.
  • Unit III (Structures, Unions & Bitfields): Nested structures, passing structures to functions, self-referential structures for linked lists, unions, memory layout differences.
  • Unit IV (File Handling in C): Streams, file pointers, sequential and random access modes (fopen, fclose, fread, fwrite, fseek, ftell, rewind).
  • Unit V (Preprocessor Directives & Macros): Macro substitution, conditional compilation (#ifdef, #ifndef), parameterized macros, header file organization.
  • Recommended Textbook: Let Us C by Yashavant Kanetkar (BPB) / Programming in ANSI C by E. Balagurusamy.

3. BCA-204: Digital Electronics & Computer Organization (DECO)

Bridges logic gates with CPU microarchitecture:

  • Unit I (Logic Gates & Boolean Simplification): Fundamental gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR), universal gate implementations, De Morgan's laws, SOP and POS forms, K-map reduction up to 5 variables.
  • Unit II (Combinational Circuits): Half adder, full adder, parallel binary adder, half/full subtractor, multiplexers (MUX), demultiplexers (DEMUX), encoders, priority encoders, decoders, and code converters (BCD to Gray).
  • Unit III (Sequential Logic Circuits): Latches, clocked flip-flops (SR, JK, D, T), race-around condition, Master-Slave JK flip-flop, excitation tables.
  • Unit IV (Registers & Counters): Shift registers (SISO, SIPO, PISO, PIPO), bidirectional shift registers, asynchronous ripple counters, synchronous binary counters, ring counters, and Johnson counters.
  • Unit V (Memory Organization): ROM, PROM, EPROM, EEPROM, static RAM vs dynamic RAM cell design, memory expansion.
  • Recommended Textbook: Digital Logic and Computer Design by M. Morris Mano (Pearson).

4. BCA-203: Organization Behavior (OB)

Examines workplace psychology, team dynamics, and human resource management:

  • Unit I (Fundamentals of OB): Historical evolution, Hawthorne studies, models of organizational behavior, challenges in modern IT organizations.
  • Unit II (Individual Behavior & Personality): Big Five personality traits, locus of control, Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI), perception errors, and attitude formation.
  • Unit III (Motivation Theories): Maslow's hierarchy of needs, Herzberg's two-factor theory, McGregor's Theory X and Theory Y, Vroom's expectancy model.
  • Unit IV (Group Dynamics & Leadership): Stages of group development (Tuckman's model), team cohesion, leadership traits, Blake and Mouton's managerial grid, conflict resolution strategies.
  • Unit V (Organizational Change & Stress): Resistance to change (Lewin's 3-stage model), organizational culture, workplace stress management techniques.
  • Recommended Textbook: Organizational Behavior by Stephen P. Robbins (Pearson).

5. BCA-205: Financial Accounting & Management

Provides financial literacy and cost accounting required for business software development:

  • Unit I (Accounting Principles): GAAP standards, business entity concept, money measurement, dual aspect principle, accounting equation.
  • Unit II (Recording Transactions): Rules of debit and credit, Journal entries, compound entries, Cash book, Sales/Purchase books, Ledger posting, and Trial Balance preparation.
  • Unit III (Preparation of Final Accounts): Manufacturing account, Trading account, Profit & Loss account, Balance sheet with adjustments (closing stock, depreciation, outstanding expenses, prepaid income).
  • Unit IV (Financial Statement Analysis): Ratio analysis: liquidity ratios (current ratio, quick ratio), solvency ratios, profitability ratios (gross profit, net profit).
  • Unit V (Working Capital & Budgeting): Components of working capital, operating cycle concept, cash budgeting, and break-even analysis.
  • Recommended Textbook: Financial Accounting for Management by Dr. S.N. Maheshwari (Vikas Publishing).

6. BCA-206: Computer Laboratory (Advanced 'C' Practical Lab)

Practical programming lab and viva-voce evaluation (50 Marks) covering advanced procedural software engineering:

  • Core Lab Experiments: Dynamic array allocation, matrix multiplication using pointers, string manipulation without string.h functions, nested structures for student/employee databases, text and binary file operations (fopen, fread, fwrite, fseek), bitwise flags.
  • Lab Viva Focus: Pointer arithmetic rules, memory leaks, malloc vs calloc, structure padding, and error handling in file streams.
