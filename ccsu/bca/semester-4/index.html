CCSU BCA 4th Semester Examination Blueprint

Official University Theory Marking Pattern

External University Theory (75 Marks) + Internal Assessment (25 Marks) for BCA-401, 402, 403, 404 & 406
Duration: 3 Hours

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (Mandatory)

5 Qs × 3 Marks = 15 Marks

Mandatory 5 short questions covering OS scheduling definitions, SDLC phases, CG formulas, and Simplex concepts.

Section B: Short Answer Questions

Attempt 2 out of 3 Qs = 15 Marks

Problem-solving questions including CPU scheduling Gantt charts, 2D matrix transformations, and Transportation tables (7.5 marks each).

Section C: Detailed Long Answer Questions

Attempt 3 out of 5 Qs = 45 Marks

Long derivations, Banker's deadlock avoidance, SRS/COCOMO case problems, Simplex iterations, and Fourier transforms (15 marks each). BCA-405 Practical lab is evaluated separately.
