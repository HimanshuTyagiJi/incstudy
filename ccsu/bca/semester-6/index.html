CCSU BCA 6th Semester Evaluation Blueprint

Theory & Major Project Evaluation Structure

Theory Papers BCA-601 to BCA-604 (75 External + 25 Internal) plus Major Project & Seminar
Theory: 3 Hours

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (Mandatory)

5 Qs × 3 Marks = 15 Marks

Mandatory conceptual questions across Network Security, ISAD, E-Commerce, and Knowledge Management.

Section B: Short Answer Questions

Attempt 2 out of 3 Qs = 15 Marks

Analytical answers covering cipher workflows, DFDs, EDI architecture, and KM conversion modes (7.5 marks each).

Section C: Detailed Long Answer Questions

Attempt 3 out of 5 Qs = 45 Marks

Comprehensive long answers on RSA/DES algorithms, SDLC implementation, online payment protocols, and KM systems (15 marks each). Major Project (BCA-605) & Presentation/Seminar (BCA-606) are assessed via project submission and external viva.

Detailed Unit-Wise Syllabus & Subject Guide

1. BCA-601: Computer Network Security

Covers principles of cryptography, secure communication channels, authentication protocols, and defense mechanisms:

  • Unit I (Security Concepts & Classical Ciphers): Security goals (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability - CIA triad), security attacks (passive vs active), vulnerabilities, classical substitution (Caesar, Playfair, Hill cipher) and transposition ciphers.
  • Unit II (Symmetric Key Cryptography): Stream ciphers vs Block ciphers, Data Encryption Standard (DES - Feistel cipher structure, key schedule), Double DES, Triple DES, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) rounds and transformations.
  • Unit III (Asymmetric Key Cryptography & Key Exchange): Principles of public-key cryptosystems, RSA algorithm (key generation, encryption, decryption, mathematical modular arithmetic), Diffie-Hellman key exchange algorithm, man-in-the-middle attacks.
  • Unit IV (Authentication & Hash Functions): Message Authentication Codes (MAC), cryptographic hash functions (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256), Digital Signatures (DSS standard), Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), digital certificates (X.509 standard).
  • Unit V (Network Defense & Web Security): IPsec architecture (AH, ESP), SSL/TLS handshake protocol, email security (PGP, S/MIME), firewalls (packet filtering, stateful inspection, application gateway), Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS).
  • Recommended Textbook: Cryptography and Network Security: Principles and Practice by William Stallings (Pearson).

2. BCA-602: Information System Analysis, Design & Implementation (ISAD)

Teaches the end-to-end engineering methodology for enterprise software systems and business processes:

  • Unit I (System Concepts & Role of Systems Analyst): System definition, characteristics (boundary, interface, feedback), types of systems (open vs closed, deterministic vs probabilistic), responsibilities and ethical roles of a systems analyst.
  • Unit II (Systems Planning & Feasibility Analysis): Problem definition, request evaluation, feasibility study (technical, economic, operational, schedule feasibility), cost-benefit analysis (payback period, Net Present Value - NPV).
  • Unit III (Structured Analysis Tools): Fact-finding techniques (interviews, questionnaires, document review), Data Flow Diagrams (Context Level 0, Level 1, Level 2 DFD conventions), Data Dictionary notation, Structured English, Decision Tables, Decision Trees.
  • Unit IV (System Design & Database Modeling): Logical design vs physical design, input design (form layouts, data validation), output design, modular system design, database table structures, normalization.
  • Unit V (System Implementation & Maintenance): Testing strategies (unit, integration, system, user acceptance), software conversion methods (Direct cutover, Parallel running, Pilot study, Phased approach), post-implementation audit, maintenance types (corrective, adaptive, perfective).
  • Recommended Textbook: Analysis and Design of Information Systems by James A. Senn (McGraw Hill).

3. BCA-603: E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce)

Analyzes electronic business architectures, internet payment protocols, supply chains, and cyber legal frameworks:

  • Unit I (Foundations of E-Commerce): Traditional commerce vs E-Commerce, driving forces, advantages and limitations, categories of E-Commerce (B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B, G2C), revenue models (advertising, subscription, transaction fee, affiliate).
  • Unit II (E-Commerce Infrastructure & EDI): Internet client-server architecture, Electronic Data Interchange (EDI - components, EDI standards ANSI X12 and EDIFACT, benefits of paperless data interchange).
  • Unit III (Electronic Payment Systems - EPS): Traditional payment systems vs online payments, credit card processing, digital wallets (e-wallets), smart cards, UPI, payment gateways, Secure Electronic Transaction (SET) protocol.
  • Unit IV (Online Marketing & Supply Chain Management): E-marketing techniques, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), banner advertising, customer relationship management (e-CRM), electronic supply chain management (e-SCM).
  • Unit V (Cyber Laws & The Information Technology Act): Legal issues in e-commerce, digital signatures legal validity, intellectual property rights, overview of India's Information Technology (IT) Act 2000, cybercrimes and penalties.
  • Recommended Textbook: E-Commerce: Business, Technology, Society by Kenneth C. Laudon & Carol Guercio Traver (Pearson).

4. BCA-604: Knowledge Management

Covers corporate knowledge assets, capture mechanisms, artificial intelligence integration, and organizational learning:

  • Unit I (Introduction to Knowledge Management): Data vs Information vs Knowledge, tacit knowledge vs explicit knowledge, knowledge hierarchy, organizational memory, drivers of knowledge management.
  • Unit II (Knowledge Management Life Cycle): KM cycle models (Zack model, Bukowitz & Williams model), knowledge creation, knowledge codification, knowledge sharing and dissemination.
  • Unit III (Knowledge Conversion Models): Nonaka & Takeuchi's SECI model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization), knowledge spirals.
  • Unit IV (Knowledge Capture & Tools): Knowledge capture techniques, expert interviews, concept mapping, knowledge repositories, collaborative workspaces, intranet portals.
  • Unit V (AI & Future Trends in KM): Role of Artificial Intelligence, expert systems, neural networks, natural language processing in knowledge extraction, measuring KM success (Balanced Scorecard approach).
  • Recommended Textbook: Knowledge Management by Elias M. Awad & Hassan M. Ghaziri (Pearson).

5. BCA-605 & BCA-606: Major Project & Seminar Guidelines

The final semester requires the completion of an industry-grade software capstone project:

  • Project Synopsis Submission: Problem statement, proposed technology stack (e.g. Full-Stack Web, Java Desktop, Flutter Mobile, Python/Django, MERN), database ER diagram, and module breakdown approved by the college project guide.
  • System Implementation: Development of clean, documented source code, responsive user interfaces, and robust backend database operations.
  • Final Project Report & External Viva: Formal spiral/hard-bound report following IEEE formatting (SRS, DFD, DB schemas, test cases, screenshots), evaluated through an in-person live software demo and external university viva voce.
