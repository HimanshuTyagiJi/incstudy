CCSU BCA 6th Semester Evaluation Blueprint

Theory & Major Project Evaluation Structure

Theory Papers BCA-601 to BCA-604 (75 External + 25 Internal) plus Major Project & Seminar
Theory: 3 Hours

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (Mandatory)

5 Qs × 3 Marks = 15 Marks

Mandatory conceptual questions across Network Security, ISAD, E-Commerce, and Knowledge Management.

Section B: Short Answer Questions

Attempt 2 out of 3 Qs = 15 Marks

Analytical answers covering cipher workflows, DFDs, EDI architecture, and KM conversion modes (7.5 marks each).

Section C: Detailed Long Answer Questions

Attempt 3 out of 5 Qs = 45 Marks

Comprehensive long answers on RSA/DES algorithms, SDLC implementation, online payment protocols, and KM systems (15 marks each). Major Project (BCA-605) & Presentation/Seminar (BCA-606) are assessed via project submission and external viva.
