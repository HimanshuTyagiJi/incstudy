How to Practice Past Year Papers in 3-Hour Timed Sets

To maximize your score on the 75-mark university external examination, allocate your 180 minutes strategically:

  • First 20 Minutes (Section A): Complete all 5 compulsory short answers (3 marks each). Keep them concise (50–75 words), highlighting precise definitions, keywords, and truth tables.
  • Next 45 Minutes (Section B): Solve your 2 selected analytical questions (7.5 marks each). Draw neat flowcharts, trace variable tables, or state step-by-step arithmetic conversions.
  • Remaining 105 Minutes (Section C): Dedicate ~35 minutes to each of your 3 chosen long questions (15 marks each). Include complete code syntax, system block diagrams, complexity analysis, and real-world advantages.
  • Final 10 Minutes: Review question numbering, underline key technical terms, and check program curly braces and semicolons.
