CCSU BCA Academic Regulations & Degree Scheme

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) program at Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), Meerut is a structured three-year full-time professional degree course spanning six semesters with a cumulative evaluation of 3,600 marks:

1. Examination & Evaluation Model

Each theoretical course in the CCSU BCA curriculum carries 100 marks divided as follows:

  • External University Examination (75 Marks): Administered at the conclusion of each semester across authorized university exam centers. Duration is 3 hours. Comprises Section A (5 compulsory questions × 3 = 15 marks), Section B (attempt 2 of 3 questions × 7.5 = 15 marks), and Section C (attempt 3 of 5 long questions × 15 = 45 marks).
  • Internal Sessional Assessment (25 Marks): Conducted continuously by affiliated college departments. Evaluated on the basis of two midterm tests (15 marks), classroom attendance (5 marks), and home assignments/class participation (5 marks).

2. Laboratory Practical & Viva Voce Scheme

Practical coursework is tested through dedicated university lab examinations conducted by external examiners appointed by CCSU:

  • Semester 1 Lab (BCA-106P): Computer Laboratory & Practical Work of C Programming (100 Marks: 75 External Practical/Viva + 25 Sessional Record).
  • Semester 3 Labs: BCA-306P (Computer Laboratory & Practical Work of DS) and BCA-307P (Computer Laboratory & Practical Work of C++).
  • Students must maintain certified, signed lab record files and execute assigned programs on lab terminals during external viva evaluation.

3. Passing Criteria & Back-Paper Provisions

To successfully pass a subject and earn academic credit:

  • A candidate must obtain at least 40% marks in aggregate (theory external + internal combined) in each individual subject;
  • Candidates failing in up to two theory papers in an academic year are eligible to appear in University Back Paper examinations conducted alongside subsequent semester cycles;
  • Final degree division awards: First Division with Distinction (75%+), First Division (60%+), Second Division (48% to 59.9%), and Pass Division (40% to 47.9%).
