Understanding State University Curriculum Mappings

Undergraduate computer science programs in India, particularly the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), share a standard core curriculum covering programming principles, data structures, discrete mathematics, computer architecture, and database management. However, individual universities introduce specific differences:

  • Subject Ordering by Semester: Some universities teach C Programming in Semester 1 and C++ in Semester 2, whereas others (such as CCSU Meerut) place C Programming in Semester 1 (BCA-102), Advanced C in Semester 2 (BCA-202), and OOP with C++ in Semester 3 (BCA-301).
  • Examination Paper Codes & Section Weightages: While universities like CCSU Meerut follow a structured 75-mark external theory paper (Section A: 15 Marks, Section B: 15 Marks, Section C: 45 Marks) plus 25 marks sessional assessment, other state universities may utilize an 80/20 or 70/30 distribution.
  • Practical Lab Examination Ratios: Lab subjects typically carry separate marks evaluated via external viva voce, code execution on university computers, and certified practical record files.

Spotlight: Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), Meerut

Chaudhary Charan Singh University (formerly Meerut University) is a premier state university established in 1965 in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. It oversees hundreds of affiliated colleges across western Uttar Pradesh offering professional degree programs in Computer Applications, Management, and Science.

IncStudy's primary active curriculum is specifically mapped to the official CCSU BCA syllabus:

  • Semester 1 (Odd): BCA-101 (Mathematics-I), BCA-102 (C Programming & Algorithms), BCA-103 (Principles of Management), BCA-104 (Business Communication), BCA-105 (Computer Fundamentals & Office Automation).
  • Semester 3 (Odd): BCA-301 (Object-Oriented Programming with C++), BCA-302 (Data Structures Using C & C++), BCA-303 (Computer Architecture & Assembly), BCA-304 (Business Economics), BCA-305 (Elements of Statistics).
