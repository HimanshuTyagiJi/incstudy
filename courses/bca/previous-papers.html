B.C.A. Dec 2015 (BCA-101 New Syllabus) · 75 Marks · 3 Hours

Section-A (Very Short Answer — 3 Marks Each · All Compulsory)
  • Beta & Gamma Functions: Define beta and gamma functions ($\beta(m, n)$ and $\Gamma(n)$ formulas and domain).
  • Matrix Equation (✓ Ticked): Find matrix $X$ such that $2A + B + X = 0$, where $A = \begin{bmatrix}-1 & 2\\3 & 4\end{bmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{bmatrix}3 & -2\\1 & 5\end{bmatrix}$.
  • Taylor's Series: Expand $\cos x$ in powers of $(x - \pi/4)$ upto 4 terms.
  • Logarithmic Differentiation: If $y = x^{x^2}$, find $\frac{dy}{dx}$.
  • Indeterminate Form Limits: Evaluate $\lim_{x \to 1} \left( \frac{1}{\ln x} - \frac{1}{x - 1} \right)$.
Section-B (Short Answer — 7.5 Marks Each · Attempt Any 2)
  • (a) Eigenvalues (✓ Ticked): Find the characteristic roots of the matrix $A = \begin{bmatrix}a & h & g\\0 & b & f\\0 & 0 & c\end{bmatrix}$.

(b) Adjoint Determinant: If $A = \begin{bmatrix}a & b\\c & d\end{bmatrix}$, find $\det(A \cdot (\text{Adj } A))$.

  • Vector Linear Dependence: Examine linear dependence of vectors $X_1 = (0, -4, 1)$, $X_2 = (2, 2, -3)$, $X_3 = (0, -4, 1)$. If dependent, find the relation between them.
  • (a) Vector Triple Product (✓ Ticked): If $A = 3\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$, $B = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}$ and $C = \hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$, find $(A \times B) \times C$.

(b) Maxima & Minima Application: Prove that the area of rectangle of given perimeter is maximum when it is a square.

Section-C (Detailed Long Answer — 15 Marks Each · Attempt Any 3)
  • (a) Continuity Condition: Find value of $a$ if $f(x) = \begin{cases}2x-1, & x \lt 2\\a, & x=2\\x+1, & x \gt 2\end{cases}$ is continuous at $x = 2$.

(b) Discontinuity Analysis: For $f(x) = \begin{cases}\frac{\|x-2\|}{2-x}, & x \neq 2\\-1, & x = 2\end{cases}$, find whether $f$ is continuous at $x = 2$ and identify the discontinuity.

  • (a) Partial Fractions Integration: Evaluate $\int \frac{3x + 1}{(x - 1)^2 (x + 3)} \, dx$.

(b) Quadratic Radical Integration: Evaluate $\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{(x + 1)(x - 2)}} \, dx$.

  • Leibniz's Theorem (✓ Ticked): If $y = e^{m \cos^{-1} x}$, show that $(1 - x^2) y_{n+2} - (2n + 1)x y_{n+1} - (n^2 + m^2) y_n = 0$ and find $y_n(0)$.
  • (a) Cardioid Curve Tracing (✓ Ticked): Trace the polar curve $r = a(1 + \cos\theta)$.

(b) Reduction Formula: Evaluate the reduction formula for $\int \cos^n x \, dx$.

  • (a) Row Operations Matrix Inverse: By using elementary row transformations, find the inverse of $A = \begin{bmatrix}5 & 2\\2 & 1\end{bmatrix}$.

(b) Cramer's Rule: Solve the system:

$$\begin{aligned} 6x + y - 3z &= 5 \\ x + 3y - 2z &= 5 \\ 2x + y + 4z &= 8 \end{aligned}$$

B.C.A. Dec 2016 (BCA-101 New Syllabus) · 75 Marks · 3 Hours

Section-A (Very Short Answer — 3 Marks Each · All Compulsory)
  • Nilpotent Matrix: Show that $A = \begin{bmatrix}ab & b^2\\-a^2 & -ab\end{bmatrix}$ is a nilpotent matrix of order 2 (i.e. $A^2 = 0$).
  • Chain Rule Differentiation: If $y = \log[\log(\log x)]$, find $\frac{dy}{dx}$.
  • Exponential Integration: Evaluate $\int \frac{(a^x - b^x)^2}{a^x b^x} \, dx$.
  • Area of Parallelogram (✓ Ticked): Calculate the area of parallelogram spanned by vectors $a = (3, -3, 1)$ and $b = (4, 9, 2)$ via $|a \times b|$.
  • Series Limit: Evaluate $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\Sigma n^2}{n^3} = \frac{1}{3}$.
Section-B (Short Answer — 7.5 Marks Each · Attempt Any 2)
  • Matrix Rank: Find the rank of the $3 \times 4$ matrix $A = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 3 & 2\\2 & 3 & 5 & 1\\1 & 3 & 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}$ using Echelon form.
  • Partial Fractions Integration (✓✓ Double Ticked · Exact Repeat): Evaluate $\int \frac{3x + 1}{(x - 1)^2 (x + 3)} \, dx$. *(Repeated from 2015 Sec-C Q10a)*
  • Maclaurin's Expansion: Expand $\sin x$ in powers of $x$ and hence find $\sin 18^\circ$ up to 4 decimal places.
Section-C (Detailed Long Answer — 15 Marks Each · Attempt Any 3)
  • (a) Limit Non-existence: Show that $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{1/x} - 1}{e^{1/x} + 1}$ does not exist (LHL = -1, RHL = 1).

(b) Continuity & Discontinuity Type: Examine continuity at $x = 0$ for $f(x) = \begin{cases}\frac{e^{1/x} - e^{-1/x}}{e^{1/x} + e^{-1/x}}, & x \neq 0\\0, & x = 0\end{cases}$ and identify discontinuity.

  • Cayley-Hamilton Theorem & Inverse (✓ Ticked): State Cayley-Hamilton theorem, verify it for $A = \begin{bmatrix}2 & -1 & 1\\-1 & 2 & -1\\1 & -1 & 2\end{bmatrix}$, and compute $A^{-1}$.
  • Algebraic Curve Tracing: Trace the curve $y^2 = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)$.
  • (a) Definite Integral Reduction Formula (✓ Ticked): If $I_n = \int_0^{\pi/4} \tan^n x \, dx$, prove $I_{n-1} + I_{n+1} = \frac{1}{n}$ and deduce $I_5$.

(b) Integration by Parts Trick: Evaluate $\int \frac{x e^x}{(x + 1)^2} \, dx = \frac{e^x}{x+1} + C$.

  • Leibniz's Theorem - Successive Differentiation (✓ Ticked): If $y = \sin(m \sin^{-1} x)$ with $|x| \lt 1$, prove:

(a) $(1 - x^2) y_2 - x y_1 + m^2 y = 0$

(b) $(1 - x^2) y_{n+2} - (2n + 1)x y_{n+1} + (m^2 - n^2) y_n = 0$ and calculate $y_n(0)$.

B.C.A. Dec 2017 (BCA-101 New Syllabus) · 75 Marks · 3 Hours

Section-A (Very Short Answer — 3 Marks Each · All Compulsory)

  • Beta & Gamma Functions Definition: Define gamma and beta function. *(Repeat from 2015 Sec-A Q1!)*

Section-B (Short Answer — 7.5 Marks Each · Attempt Any 2)

  • Leibniz's Product Rule: Find the $n$th differential coefficient of $x^3 \cos x$.
  • Vector Area of Rectangle: Find area of rectangle with vertices $a = (-1, 1/2, 4)$, $b = (1, 1/2, 4)$, $c = (1, -1/2, 4)$, $d = (-1, -1/2, 4)$.
Section-C (Detailed Long Answer — 15 Marks Each · Attempt Any 3)
  • Cayley-Hamilton Theorem & Vectors (✓ Ticked): Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for $A = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 2 & 1\\1 & 3 & 1\\1 & 2 & 2\end{bmatrix}$, and find characteristic roots & eigenvectors. *(Annual 15M repeat!)*
  • Multi-interval Continuity: Examine continuity of $f(x)$ at $x = 0, 1, 2$ across 4 definitions ($-x^2$, $5x-4$, $4x^2-3x$, $3x+4$).
  • Folium of Descartes: Trace the curve $x^3 + y^3 = 3axy$ (symmetry about $y=x$, loop in first quadrant, asymptote $x+y+a=0$).
  • (a) Maclaurin Expansion: Expand $\log(1 + x)$ by Maclaurin's Theorem.

(b) Taylor Expansion: Expand $\sin x$ in powers of $(x - \pi/2)$ by Taylor's Theorem.

  • Integration Techniques (✓ Ticked Both):

(a) (✓ Ticked) $\int \frac{e^x(1+x)}{\cos^2(e^x x)} \, dx = \tan(x e^x) + C$.

(b) (✓ Ticked) $\int \sin x \cdot \sin 2x \cdot \sin 3x \, dx$.

B.C.A. Dec 2018 (BCA-101 New Syllabus) · 75 Marks · 3 Hours

Section-A (Very Short Answer — 3 Marks Each · All Compulsory)
  • Hermitian Matrix Proof (✓ Ticked): Show that $A = \begin{bmatrix}3 & 1+2i\\1-2i & 2\end{bmatrix}$ is Hermitian ($A^\theta = A$). *(2nd consecutive year!)*
  • Limit Definition: Define limit of a function at a point ($\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$).
  • Hyperbola Asymptotes: Find asymptotes of the curve $\frac{a^2}{x^2} - \frac{b^2}{y^2} = 1$ ($x^2 y^2 - a^2 y^2 + b^2 x^2 = 0 \implies x = \pm a$).
  • Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: State the fundamental theorem of calculus ($\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)dt = f(x)$).
  • 2D Vector Definition: Define vector in 2-dimension with example ($\vec{v} = v_x \hat{i} + v_y \hat{j}$).
Section-B (Short Answer — 7.5 Marks Each · Attempt Any 2)
  • Continuous Function Parameters: Determine values of $a, b, c$ such that $f(x)$ is continuous at $x = 0$ ($c = 1/2, a = -3/2, b \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$).
  • Leibniz's Theorem - Successive Differentiation (✓ Ticked): If $y = a\cos(\log x) + b\sin(\log x)$, show $x^2 y_2 + x y_1 + y = 0$ and $x^2 y_{n+2} + (2n+1)x y_{n+1} + (n^2+1)y_n = 0$. *(Leibniz's Theorem 4 years running!)*
  • Area of Parallelogram (✓ Ticked): Calculate area of parallelogram spanned by vectors $a = (1, -1, 3)$ and $b = (2, -7, 1)$ via $|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = 15\sqrt{2}$ units. *(Repeat of 2016 Sec-A Q4!)*
Section-C (Detailed Long Answer — 15 Marks Each · Attempt Any 3)
  • Cayley-Hamilton Theorem & Vectors (✓ Ticked): Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for $A = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 2\\2 & 1 & 2\\2 & 2 & 1\end{bmatrix}$. Find characteristic roots ($\lambda = -1, -1, 5$) and eigenvectors. *(4th consecutive year 15M question!)*
  • Continuity Tests at $x = 0$:

(a) $f(x) = \frac{|x|}{x}$ for $x \neq 0, f(0) = 0$ (Discontinuous, jump discontinuity).

(b) $f(x) = \frac{e^{1/x} \sin(1/x)}{1 + e^{1/x}}$ for $x \neq 0, f(0) = 0$ (Continuous at $x = 0$).

  • Loop Curve Tracing: Trace the curve $9a y^2 = (x - 2a)(x - 5a)^2$ (loop between $2a$ and $5a$, node at $(5a, 0)$).
  • (a) Maclaurin Expansion: Expand $\frac{e^x}{e^x + 1}$ by Maclaurin's Theorem.

(b) Taylor Expansion: Expand $\log x$ in powers of $(x - 1)$ by Taylor's Theorem ($= (x-1) - \frac{(x-1)^2}{2} + \frac{(x-1)^3}{3} - \dots$).

  • Integration (✓ Ticked Both):

(a) (✓ Ticked) $\int \tan^4 x \, dx = \frac{\tan^3 x}{3} - \tan x + x + C$.

(b) (✓ Ticked) $\int \frac{\cos 2x - \cos 2\alpha}{\cos x - \cos \alpha} \, dx = 2\sin x + 2x \cos\alpha + C$.

  • Rank Definition (✓ Ticked): Define rank of a matrix (maximum number of linearly independent row/column vectors).

  • Rolle's Theorem Verification: Verify Rolle's theorem for $f(x) = 2x^3 + x^2 - 4x - 2$ on $[-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2}]$. ($f'(x) = 0 \implies x = -1, 2/3 \in (-\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$).

Section-B (Short Answer — 7.5 Marks Each · Attempt Any 2)

Section-C (Detailed Long Answer — 15 Marks Each · Attempt Any 3)

  • Matrix Normal Form: Reduce matrix to normal form $[I_r, 0]$ and determine its rank.
